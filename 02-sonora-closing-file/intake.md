# Intake — Sonora Closing File

Asked at fee confirmation, before any work starts.

---

## 1. Documents, whatever exists

| Document | Why it matters | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| Trust agreement (*fideicomiso*) or draft deed | The whole vehicle analysis | Findings 2–4 become "cannot assess", which is itself a finding |
| Public registry certificate | Who actually owns it | Order one — it is the single cheapest document in the file |
| Prior deed / deed of acquisition | Chain of title | Chain cannot be traced; stated as a limitation |
| Foreign investment permit | Scope versus actual use | Finding 5 limited |
| Purchase agreement | Obligations, deposits, deadlines | Cannot key actions to the closing date |
| Property tax, water, HOA statements | Encumbrances | Listed as not verified |
| Survey or plat, if any | Restricted-zone measurement | Zone status stated as reported, not verified |

Scans or clear phone photos are fine. Note the quality in the limitations if poor.

---

## 2. Buyer facts that change the answer

- Age bracket and **expected holding period** — drives the vehicle recommendation more than anything else
- **Intended use**: residence, rental, mixed, commercial
- Whether more Mexican property is planned
- State of residence and citizenship
- Whether they already hold a Mexican tax ID
- Marital property regime and who is intended to inherit
- **One line: what already worries them.** Same rule as the Spanish product — that line tells you which finding is the one they will actually read.

---

## 3. Identification

Passport data page, and for the vehicle product (B), the U.S. entity's formation documents and
the identity of the individuals who ultimately control it.

⚠ Where the engagement extends into structuring or the organization of contributions for the
formation or operation of a company, the Mexican anti-money-laundering framework may apply.
Verify the scope of article 17, section XI of the LFPIORPI and its thresholds against the text
in force before fixing the final policy. Collect identification from day one regardless — it is
free at intake and expensive later.

---

## 4. Written before work starts

1. **Engagement letter**, one page: scope, the twelve points, five business days from the last
   document, flat fee in USD, what is excluded, and the statement that this is not title
   insurance and not representation in the transaction.
2. **Privacy notice** acknowledgment, with retention and destruction policy.

---

## 5. The clock

Five business days run **from the last document received**, not from payment. Say it in the
engagement letter and it never becomes a conversation.
